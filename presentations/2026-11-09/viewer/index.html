Slot Attention on DINO patches: one RGB-D frame → instances → superquadrics

Overfit one frameGeneralization

Losses

Slots are matched one-to-one to the GT instances by their masks (Hungarian), and each slot's parts to the instance's target superquadrics by their Chamfer distance (Hungarian). All terms have weight 1.

Maskcross-entropy

$$\mathcal{L}_\text{mask} = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n}\sum_{m} s_{nm}\,\log a_{n\,\sigma(m)}$$

Diceper matched slot

$$\mathcal{L}_\text{dice} = 1-\frac{2\,|A\cap S|}{|A|+|S|}$$

Existenceslots and parts, BCE

$$\mathcal{L}_\text{exist} = \mathrm{BCE}(p,\,y),\qquad y = 1 \text{ if matched, else } 0$$

Chamferper matched part and target SQ

$$\mathcal{L}_\text{CD} = \frac{1}{|P|}\sum_{x\in P}\min_{y\in Q}\lVert x-y\rVert + \frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_{y\in Q}\min_{x\in P}\lVert x-y\rVert$$

Total

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_\text{mask} + \mathcal{L}_\text{dice} + \mathcal{L}^\text{slot}_\text{exist} + \mathcal{L}_\text{CD} + \mathcal{L}^\text{part}_\text{exist}$$
